# Privacy Policy — My Allergies Scanner

**Last updated:** November 10, 2026  
**Developer:** insanyya-dev  
**Contact:** insanyya.support@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies to both versions of the app on Google Play:

- **My Allergies Scanner** (Free)
- **My Allergies Scanner Premium**

By using the app you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

---

## 1. Overview

My Allergies Scanner shows whether a food product contains the allergens **you and the people you shop for** need to avoid, by scanning its barcode or photographing its ingredient list. The app informs; it is not medical advice.

**Your allergies are health information. They stay on your phone.** We have no server that receives them, no account and no login. Nobody at insanyya-dev can see who you are, what you avoid or what you scan.

The app connects to the internet only to:

- fetch product information from the OpenFoodFacts open food database;
- load ads and the consent form (Free version only).

This policy explains exactly what data is involved, how it is used, and your rights over it.

---

## 2. Data we collect and how it is used

### 2.1 Your people and their allergies (stored only on your device)

To check products, the app keeps on your device, for you and each person you add:

- a first name (optional; without one the app says "you") and a colour;
- the allergens and intolerances to avoid, and how strict to be with each (for example "may contain");
- your own words to avoid (for example "fennel");
- how the allergy card speaks about this person (for example "My son").

This data **stays on your device**. It is never sent to us, to OpenFoodFacts or to Google. It is used only to check products for these people.

### 2.2 Products, baskets and lists (stored only on your device)

When you scan or open a product, the app saves to a local database on your device:

- the product barcode, name and brand, and its ingredient information from OpenFoodFacts;
- the result for each person (for example "Not for Emma: contains milk") and the scan date;
- your Favourites and "Never again" products;
- your baskets (Premium): the products, the notes you write, and whether a result has changed;
- your shopping list (Premium): the lines you type and which ones are ticked.

If you read an ingredient list with the camera, the text read from the photo is used for the check. **The photo itself is not saved**.

You can delete products from My products, remove baskets and list lines, and delete people at any time in the app.

### 2.3 Data sent to OpenFoodFacts

When you scan a barcode, search for a product or look for alternatives, the app contacts the OpenFoodFacts API (world.openfoodfacts.org). The request contains:

- the product barcode, or your search words (for example a line of your shopping list);
- your language code (for example "fr", "en") and, for searches, a country code and the shop name if you entered one, so the results fit where you shop;
- a technical "User-Agent" that names the app, as OpenFoodFacts requires.

**Your allergies and your people are never sent.** Products are checked against them on your device, after they arrive. No personal information about you (name, email, device identifier, location) is included. OpenFoodFacts is an independent nonprofit project; its privacy policy: <https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy>

### 2.4 Camera

The app asks for camera permission to scan barcodes and to read ingredient lists.

- Barcode scanning and text reading happen **entirely on your device** (Google ML Kit, on-device). The images are **never** sent to any server.
- Photos are not saved and are never uploaded.
- The app never reads the photos in your gallery.

### 2.5 Weekly re-check and notifications (Premium)

Once a week, Premium checks the products in your recent baskets and your Favourites again, so you know if a recipe changed. This runs **on your device**: it asks OpenFoodFacts for these barcodes (as in 2.3) and compares the answer with your people's allergies on the phone. If a product got worse for someone, the app shows a notification on your phone. The app asks for notification permission the first time you add a product to a basket; you can turn notifications off in Android Settings.

### 2.6 Home-screen widget and shortcuts (Premium)

If you add the widget, it shows on your home screen who is checked (first names) and the result of your last scan. Anyone who can see your home screen can see it. Remove the widget to stop this.

### 2.7 Allergy card

The allergy card (for restaurants) is built on your device from your people's allergies and shown on your screen. It is not sent anywhere. If you choose to show it to someone, they see what is on it.

### 2.8 Moving from Free to Premium

If you move your data from the Free app to Premium, it goes **directly from one app to the other on your phone** (people, their allergies, My products). Before sending, each app checks that the other one is really ours (Google Play signature). Nothing goes through the internet or through us.

### 2.9 Settings stored on your device

The following settings are stored on your device only and never sent anywhere:

- your app language, look (light or dark) and the allergy card language;
- whether the welcome screens and the notification question were already shown;
- your advertising consent choice (Free version only).

### 2.10 Android backup

If you turned on Android backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data (your people, their allergies, your products) in **your own** Google account backup, so it comes back on a new phone. This backup is made by Android in your Google account, not by us; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

### 2.11 Advertising (Free version only)

The Free version shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. To serve them, Google may collect and process:

- the Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier);
- your IP address (used to estimate an approximate region);
- information about the ads shown and your interaction with them;
- device information (Android version, screen size, language);
- diagnostic information from the ads SDK.

This data is collected and processed **by Google, not by us**, under Google's Privacy Policy (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>) and the AdMob partner policies (<https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>). **Your allergies are never given to the ads SDK**, and ads are never chosen from them.

You can opt out of personalised ads or reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent before loading ads; you can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings → **Ad privacy choices**.

The **Premium version contains no ads**: Google AdMob is not started and no advertising data is collected.

### 2.12 Contacting us (optional)

The **Contact** option in Settings opens your own email app with our address filled in (and the app version, to help us). Your message then reaches us through your own email provider, by your own action. We use it only to answer you. We never use it for marketing, never sell it and never share it.

## 3. Data we do not collect

Apart from what you choose to send us by email (section 2.12), we do **not** collect or have access to:

- your allergies, intolerances or any health information;
- the names of the people you add;
- your scans, products, baskets or shopping list;
- your name, email address or any account;
- your location (precise or approximate);
- your contacts, calendar or messages;
- your browsing history or data from other apps;
- payment information (purchases go through Google Play; we never see payment details);
- the photos in your gallery or files on your device.

The app does not require an account or a login.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

**People, allergies, products, baskets, shopping list:** kept on your device until you delete them in the app.

**Everything at once:** clear the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → My Allergies Scanner → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the app.

**Android backup:** managed in your own Google account (see 2.10).

**Advertising data (Free version):** handled by Google under its own retention rules. You can manage it at <https://myaccount.google.com>.

**Emails you send us:** kept in our support inbox as long as needed to answer you. Write to insanyya.support@gmail.com and we will delete your messages and email address, unless the law requires us to keep them.

## 6. Your rights

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another place with privacy laws, you have the right to:

- **access** the data we hold about you;
- **correct** it;
- have it **deleted**;
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it;
- receive it in a **portable** format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time (for example for personalised ads in the Free version).

Your allergy data and your products stay on your device, so you control them yourself in the app (editing or deleting people, deleting products, clearing app data). The only data that reaches us is what you send us by email: for that, write to insanyya.support@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

For data handled by third parties (Google, OpenFoodFacts), you can also contact them directly using the links in section 4.

## 7. Children

You can add a child as one of the people you shop for; their allergies stay on your device like everyone else's (section 2.1). The app itself is meant to be used by adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to insanyya.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

In the Free version, ads are not personalised for users who have not given consent.

## 8. Security

Data on your device (people, allergies, products, settings) is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Everything the app sends over the internet (to OpenFoodFacts, to Google AdMob) uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

We do not run any server that stores personal data. The security of the data on your device also depends on your device (screen lock, encryption).

## 9. International transfers

When the app contacts OpenFoodFacts or Google AdMob, your request may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States. OpenFoodFacts is a French nonprofit with its main servers in the EU. Google operates worldwide and relies on legal transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and adequacy decisions). Your allergies are never part of these requests.
