# Privacy Policy — My Halal Scanner

**Last updated:** November 5, 2026  
**Developer:** Insanyya  
**Contact:** insanyya.support@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies to both versions of the app on Google Play:

- **My Halal Scanner** (Free)
- **My Halal Scanner Premium**

By using the app you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

---

## 1. Overview

My Halal Scanner shows whether a food product contains the ingredients **you choose to avoid** (for example pork, alcohol, gelatine or insects), by scanning its barcode or photographing its ingredient list. The app does not decide what is halal for you: every answer follows your own choices in Settings.

The app works mainly on your device, using local data files. It connects to the internet only to:

- fetch product information from the OpenFoodFacts open food database;
- load ads and the consent form (Free version only);
- deliver a message to us, if you choose to use the in-app contact form.

This policy explains exactly what data is involved, how it is used, and your rights over it.

## 2. Data we collect and how it is used

### 2.1 Data stored only on your device

When you scan a product, the app saves to a local database on your device:

- the product barcode, name and brand;
- the ingredient list (as published, and as shown to you);
- the result of the check (what was found, what may be present, what was checked);
- the ingredients that matched your choices (for example "gelatine", "E120");
- the scan date and time;
- the product image link (a link to the OpenFoodFacts image, not a copy);
- the product category and labels (for example a halal mark recorded on OpenFoodFacts).

If you choose to save an ingredient-label scan, the app also keeps the text read from the photo, the name you give it, and the photos you took (see 2.3).

This data **stays on your device**. It is never sent to us. It is used only to show your history in the app and to re-check products against your current choices. You can delete single entries or your whole history at any time from the History screen.

### 2.2 Data sent to OpenFoodFacts

When you scan a barcode or search for a product, the app contacts the OpenFoodFacts API (world.openfoodfacts.org and its country sites). The request contains:

- the product barcode, or your search words;
- your language code (for example "fr", "en", "ar") and, for searches, a country code, so the results come in your language;
- a technical "User-Agent" that names the app and gives a developer contact address (not yours), as OpenFoodFacts requires.

No personal information about you (name, email, device identifier, location) is included. OpenFoodFacts is an independent nonprofit project; its privacy policy: <https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy>

### 2.3 Camera and photos

The app asks for camera permission to:

1. scan product barcodes;
2. photograph ingredient lists so the text can be read (Free and Premium);
3. take product photos when you choose to contribute a product to OpenFoodFacts.

- Barcode scanning and text reading happen **entirely on your device** (Google ML Kit, on-device). The images are **never** sent to any server for this.
- Photos taken for ingredient scans are kept in the app's private storage (not visible to other apps or in your gallery) until you delete the scan, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- A photo is uploaded to OpenFoodFacts **only** if you choose to contribute that product and confirm it.
- The app never reads the photos already in your gallery.

### 2.4 Product contributions to OpenFoodFacts (optional)

If you choose to add or correct a product on OpenFoodFacts from the app, the following is sent to OpenFoodFacts:

- the product barcode;
- the ingredient text you entered or that was read from your photo;
- the photos you choose to upload (front, ingredients, nutrition);
- a shared contributor account of the app (not linked to your identity: all contributions from the app appear under the same account).

These contributions are published under OpenFoodFacts' open licence (ODbL) and become part of the public database. This feature is optional.

### 2.5 Settings stored on your device

The following settings are stored on your device only and never sent anywhere:

- what you choose to avoid (pork, meat without a halal mark, blood, insects, alcohol, vinegar, animal rennet, additives that may be animal) and the level you chose for each (strict or lighter);
- whether the app should take a halal mark into account;
- your own list of words to point out (Premium);
- your app language and display preferences;
- whether the welcome / "What's new" screens were already shown;
- your advertising consent choice (Free version only).

### 2.6 Advertising (Free version only)

The Free version shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. To serve them, Google may collect and process:

- the Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier);
- your IP address (used to estimate an approximate region);
- information about the ads shown and your interaction with them;
- device information (Android version, screen size, language);
- diagnostic information from the ads SDK.

This data is collected and processed **by Google, not by us**, under Google's Privacy Policy (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>) and the AdMob partner policies (<https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>).

You can opt out of personalised ads or reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent before loading ads; you can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy** button on the app's home screen.

The **Premium version contains no ads**: Google AdMob is not started and no advertising data is collected.

### 2.7 In-app contact form (optional)

If, and only if, you fill in and send the contact form, the following is sent:

- the email address you type, so we can reply to you;
- the subject and the message you write;
- the app version, the Android version and the app language (added automatically, to help us understand your report).

The phone model, device identifiers and your location are **not** sent.

The message goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to a small program we run on **Google Apps Script**, inside our own Google account, which emails it to our support inbox (**Gmail**, insanyya.support@gmail.com). Google therefore processes the message as our email provider. Like any website, Google's servers see the connection's IP address; our program does not receive it and it is never included in the email we read. We use your message only to read it and answer you. We never use it for marketing, never sell it and never share it with anyone else.

App versions released before the "Halal 2.0" update delivered this form through Formspree (a US form service, <https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy>). From that update on, Formspree is no longer used.

If you use the "Email us" or "Report an error" options instead, the app simply opens your own email app with our address filled in; your message then reaches us through your own email provider, by your own action.

Contacting us is optional. Every feature of the app works without it.

## 3. Data we do not collect

Apart from what you choose to send us through the contact form or by email (section 2.7), we do **not** collect or have access to:

- your name, email address or any account;
- your location (precise or approximate);
- your contacts, calendar or messages;
- your browsing history or data from other apps;
- health or biometric data;
- payment information (purchases go through Google Play; we never see payment details);
- the photos in your gallery or files on your device.

The app does not require an account or a login.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

**Scan history:** kept on your device until you delete it (swipe an item, or clear all from the History screen).

**Settings and photos:** deleted when you clear the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → My Halal Scanner → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the app.

**OpenFoodFacts contributions:** kept by OpenFoodFacts under its open-database rules. To ask for their deletion, contact OpenFoodFacts at contact@openfoodfacts.org.

**Advertising data (Free version):** handled by Google under its own retention rules. You can manage it at <https://myaccount.google.com>.

**Contact messages:** kept in our support inbox as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of our conversation. Write to insanyya.support@gmail.com and we will delete your messages and email address, unless the law requires us to keep them.

## 6. Your rights

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another place with privacy laws, you have the right to:

- **access** the data we hold about you;
- **correct** it;
- have it **deleted**;
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it;
- receive it in a **portable** format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time (for example for personalised ads in the Free version).

Almost all of your data stays on your device, so you can exercise most of these rights yourself in the app (deleting history, changing settings, clearing app data). The only data that reaches us is what you send through the contact form or by email: for that, write to insanyya.support@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

For data handled by third parties (Google, OpenFoodFacts), you can also contact them directly using the links in section 4.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to insanyya.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

In the Free version, ads are not personalised for users who have not given consent.

## 8. Security

Data on your device (history, settings, photos) is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Everything the app sends over the internet (to OpenFoodFacts, to our contact form, to Google AdMob) uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). The app's network settings block unencrypted (HTTP) connections.

Apart from the contact messages you choose to send us, we do not run any server that stores personal data. The security of the data on your device also depends on your device (screen lock, encryption).

## 9. International transfers

When the app contacts OpenFoodFacts, Google (AdMob, Apps Script, Gmail) or, for older versions, Formspree, your request may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States. OpenFoodFacts is a French nonprofit with its main servers in the EU. Google operates worldwide and relies on legal transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and adequacy decisions).
