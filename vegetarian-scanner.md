# Privacy Policy — My Vegetarian Scanner

**Last updated:** November 10, 2026  
**Developer:** insanyya-dev  
**Contact:** insanyya.support@gmail.com

This policy covers both Android apps: **My Vegetarian Scanner** (free, with ads) and
**My Vegetarian Scanner Premium** (paid, no ads). "We" means the developer of these apps.

## In short
- There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting. We, the developer, receive
  none of your data.
- Your settings, scans and saved products stay on your phone.
- To look up a product, the app sends its barcode (or your search words) to Open Food Facts, a
  free public food database.
- The free app shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data (see "Ads").
  Premium has no ads.
- We never sell your data.

## What stays on your phone
The app stores these on your phone only, in its private storage:
- your settings (diet profile, the ingredients you choose to avoid, your own word list, language, theme);
- your scan history, your saved products ("My products", Premium) and your scan statistics;
- the photos you take of ingredient labels or of a product's front.

We cannot see any of this.

## Camera and photos
The app uses the camera only while the scanner is open, to read barcodes and ingredient labels.
Both are read **on your phone** (Google ML Kit, on-device). The photos are not uploaded. They are
sent only if you choose to add a product to Open Food Facts (see below).

## Microphone (dictating ingredients)
If you tap the microphone to dictate an ingredient list, your phone's own speech recognition
service (for example Google's) turns your voice into text. The app receives only the text. The app
does not have the microphone permission and does not record audio itself.

## Open Food Facts
Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) is a free, public food database. The app connects to it to:
- look up a product when you scan its barcode (the barcode is sent);
- search for products (your search words, country and language are sent);
- show product photos and suggest alternatives;
- re-check your saved products about once a week (Premium; their barcodes are sent).

Like any website, Open Food Facts receives your IP address. Their privacy policy:
https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

**Adding or editing a product:** if you add a missing product or correct one, the details you
enter (name, brand, ingredients and the photos you choose) are sent to Open Food Facts through the
app's contributor account, and **published publicly** under the Open Database License, so that
everyone can use them. Please don't include people or personal information in these photos. To
remove a contribution, contact Open Food Facts.

## Ads (free app only)
The free app shows ads from **Google AdMob**. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your
device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (worked out from your IP address) and
how you interact with ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form asks for your
choice first. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's
settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads). Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Premium contains no ads and no ad code.**

## Moving your scans from the free app to Premium
"Move my scans to Premium" sends your history directly from one app to the other, on the same
phone. It doesn't go through the internet, and the free app sends it only to the genuine Premium
app (checked by its signature).

## Backups, exports and sharing
- **Export & backup (Premium):** PDF, CSV and backup files are saved where you choose. You
  control those files.
- **Android backup:** if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the
  app's data in your Google account backup, which Google encrypts. This is controlled by your
  phone's backup settings, not by us.
- **Sharing:** when you share a result, you choose the app and the person it goes to.

## Contacting us
"Contact us" and "Report a wrong result" open your own email app. The email includes the app
version, your phone model and Android version, to help us fix problems. We use your email address
only to reply.

## Deleting your data
- In the app: clear your history and remove saved products.
- All of it at once: Settings › Apps › My Vegetarian Scanner › Storage › Clear storage, or
  uninstall the app.
- Ads: reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
- Open Food Facts contributions: contact Open Food Facts.

## Children
The apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
