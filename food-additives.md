# Privacy Policy — Food Additives

**Last updated:** DRAFT, to be dated on release of version 5.0  
**Developer:** insanyya-dev  
**Contact:** insanyya.support@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Food Additives** app on Google Play, in its Free form and with Premium (a one-time purchase inside the app).

By using the app you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the app.

---

## 1. Overview

Food Additives explains food additives (E-numbers): what each one is, what official safety bodies concluded, what independent studies found, and whether it fits the diets you follow. You can also scan a product's barcode, photograph its ingredient list or type ingredients to see the additives it contains. The app informs; it is not medical advice.

**We have no server that receives your data, no account and no login.** Nobody at insanyya-dev can see who you are, what you look up or what you scan.

The additive information is built into the app and works offline. The app connects to the internet only to:

- fetch product information from the OpenFoodFacts open food database;
- load ads and the consent form (Free users only, see 2.9);
- check your Premium purchase with Google Play.

This policy explains exactly what data is involved, how it is used, and your rights over it.

---

## 2. Data we collect and how it is used

### 2.1 Your history, favourites and watched products (stored only on your device)

The app keeps in a local database on your device:

- the products you scanned (barcode, name, brand, the additives found) and the date;
- the additives you opened and the ingredient lists you typed or photographed (the text only);
- your Favourites;
- the products you chose to watch, with their additives at the last check.

This data **stays on your device**. It is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time in the app.

If you used an earlier version of the app (4.x or older), your old scan history is moved into the new history on your device the first time version 5.0 starts. Nothing leaves the phone.

### 2.2 Your settings (stored only on your device)

- your app language and look (light, dark or system);
- the diets you follow (for example vegetarian or halal), used only to mark which additives fit them;
- your daily scan counter (Free) and how many reward videos you watched today;
- whether you have Premium, and the end date of your free Premium trial;
- whether you had the app before version 5.0 (decided once, on your phone, from the install date);
- whether the welcome screens were already shown;
- your advertising consent choice (Free users only).

These settings are never sent to us.

### 2.3 Data sent to OpenFoodFacts

When you scan a barcode, or when the app checks a product you watch (2.5), the app contacts the OpenFoodFacts API (world.openfoodfacts.org). The request contains:

- the product barcode;
- your language code (for example "fr", "en"), so the product's name and ingredients come in your language;
- a technical "User-Agent" that names the app and its version, as OpenFoodFacts requires.

**Your diets, history and favourites are never sent.** Additives are found on your device, after the answer arrives. No personal information about you (name, email, device identifier, location) is included. OpenFoodFacts is an independent nonprofit project; its privacy policy: <https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy>

### 2.4 Camera

The app asks for camera permission to scan barcodes and to read ingredient lists.

- Barcode scanning and text reading happen **entirely on your device** (Google ML Kit, on-device). The images are **never** sent to any server.
- Photos are not saved and are never uploaded.
- The app never reads the photos in your gallery.

### 2.5 Watched products and notifications

If you watch a product, the app checks it again about once a week, **on your device**: it asks OpenFoodFacts for that barcode (as in 2.3) and compares the additives with the last check. If the recipe changed, the app shows a notification on your phone. The app asks for notification permission when you first watch a product; you can turn notifications off in Android Settings.

### 2.6 Premium, the free trial and purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase made through **Google Play**. The app asks Google Play which of its products you own, to unlock Premium. **We never see your payment details, name or email**; Google Play handles the payment under its own privacy policy.

New users get a free Premium trial (a few days). To make sure a trial is given only once, **the end date of the trial is also kept in Google Block Store**: a few bytes saved by Google Play services in **your own** Google account backup, so they come back if you reinstall the app. It contains only that date: no identifier, nothing about you, and it is never sent to us. It is removed if you turn off Google backup or delete your backup.

### 2.7 Android backup

If you turned on Android backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data (history, favourites, settings) in **your own** Google account backup, so it comes back on a new phone. This backup is made by Android in your Google account, not by us; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

### 2.8 Contacting us (optional)

The **Contact** option in Settings opens your own email app with our address filled in (and the app version, to help us). Your message then reaches us through your own email provider, by your own action. We use it only to answer you. We never use it for marketing, never sell it and never share it.

### 2.9 Advertising (Free users only)

Free users can see ads served by **Google AdMob**:

- **banners**, only for people who installed the app from version 5.0 on (people who had the app before never see banners);
- **reward videos**, only when you choose to watch one to get more scans.

To serve them, Google may collect and process:

- the Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier);
- your IP address (used to estimate an approximate region);
- information about the ads shown and your interaction with them;
- device information (Android version, screen size, language);
- diagnostic information from the ads SDK.

This data is collected and processed **by Google, not by us**, under Google's Privacy Policy (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>) and the AdMob partner policies (<https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543>). **Your history, diets and scans are never given to the ads SDK**, and ads are never chosen from them.

You can opt out of personalised ads or reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent before loading ads; you can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings → **Ad privacy choices**.

**With Premium, during the free trial, and for anyone who gets no ads, Google AdMob is not started and no advertising data is collected.** The ads SDK stays in the app but stays inactive.

## 3. Data we do not collect

Apart from what you choose to send us by email (section 2.8), we do **not** collect or have access to:

- your history, favourites, watched products or scans;
- your diets or any health information;
- your name, email address or any account;
- your location (precise or approximate);
- your contacts, calendar or messages;
- your browsing history or data from other apps;
- payment information (purchases go through Google Play; we never see payment details);
- the photos in your gallery or files on your device.

The app does not require an account or a login.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

**History, favourites, watched products, settings:** kept on your device until you delete them in the app.

**Everything at once:** clear the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → Food Additives → Storage → Clear data) or uninstall the app.

**Trial end date in Block Store and Android backup:** kept in your own Google account backup (see 2.6 and 2.7); you control it in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

**Advertising data (Free users):** handled by Google under its own retention rules. You can manage it at <https://myaccount.google.com>.

**Emails you send us:** kept in our support inbox as long as needed to answer you. Write to insanyya.support@gmail.com and we will delete your messages and email address, unless the law requires us to keep them.

## 6. Your rights

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another place with privacy laws, you have the right to:

- **access** the data we hold about you;
- **correct** it;
- have it **deleted**;
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use it;
- receive it in a **portable** format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time (for example for personalised ads).

Your history and settings stay on your device, so you control them yourself in the app (deleting history, favourites, watched products, clearing app data). The only data that reaches us is what you send us by email: for that, write to insanyya.support@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

For data handled by third parties (Google, OpenFoodFacts), you can also contact them directly using the links in section 4.

## 7. Children

The app is meant to be used by adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to insanyya.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Ads are not personalised for users who have not given consent.

## 8. Security

Data on your device (history, favourites, settings) is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Everything the app sends over the internet (to OpenFoodFacts, Google Play, Google AdMob) uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

We do not run any server that stores personal data. The security of the data on your device also depends on your device (screen lock, encryption).

## 9. International transfers

When the app contacts OpenFoodFacts or Google, your request may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States. OpenFoodFacts is a French nonprofit with its main servers in the EU. Google operates worldwide and relies on legal transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and adequacy decisions).
